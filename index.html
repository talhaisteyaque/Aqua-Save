Educational Knowledge

Water-Saving Tips & Facts

Explore practical, evidence-based water efficiency tips across household categories and grounded environmental facts.

Bathroom High Impact

The 5-Minute Shower Challenge

Shortening your daily shower from 10 minutes to 5 minutes conserves approximately 47 Litres of water every single day—over 17,000 Litres annually per person.

Bathroom Zero-Cost Habit

Turn Off the Tap While Brushing

Letting the faucet run while brushing your teeth wastes roughly 6 Litres per minute. Shutting it off during your two-minute brushing routine saves up to 12 Litres each session.

Kitchen High Impact

Two-Basin Dishwashing Method

Instead of letting the tap run continuously, fill one basin or bowl with soapy water for washing and another with clean water for rinsing, saving over 40 Litres per day.

Laundry Medium Impact

Consolidate to Full Loads

Washing machines consume approximately 50 Litres of water per cycle regardless of fullness. Running only full loads cuts weekly water and electricity consumption by 25%–30%.

Gardening High Impact

Water Plants at Dawn or Dusk

Watering during peak daytime heat results in up to 40% loss to solar evaporation. Watering in early morning or late evening ensures deep soil moisture absorption.

Toilet Medium Impact

Cistern Displacement Bottle

For older single-flush toilets (9L), placing a sealed 1-litre plastic bottle filled with sand inside the cistern displaces 1 Litre per flush, saving 1,800+ Litres yearly.

Household Critical Impact

Perform Overnight Leak Audits

Read your household water meter before retiring to bed when no water fixtures are operating. If the meter reading advances by morning, a hidden plumbing leak exists.

Kitchen Greywater Reuse

Reuse Produce Rinsing Water

Rinse vegetables and fruits in a large bowl instead of under a running stream. The captured greywater is chemical-free and ideal for watering indoor or balcony plants.

Gardening Soil Moisture

Apply Organic Mulch to Garden Beds

Adding a 2-inch layer of organic mulch (wood chips, leaves, or compost) around plants reduces soil evaporation by 70% and suppresses moisture-competing weeds.

Did You Know? Environmental Water Facts

01

Only 0.5% Freshwater is Accessible

While water covers 71% of Earth's surface, 97% is saline ocean water and over 2% is locked in glaciers. Less than 0.5% is available as accessible freshwater.

02

A Slow Drip Wastes 9,000+ Litres

A tap leaking at just one drop per second wastes roughly 25 Litres of treated municipal water each day, accumulating to over 9,000 Litres in a single year.

03

Water & Energy Nexus

Pumping, treating, heating, and filtering household water consumes significant electrical energy. Conserving hot water directly slashes household greenhouse gas emissions.

About Project: AquaSave

Water Conservation Recommendation System | Functional Working Prototype

Problem Statement & Context

Freshwater scarcity is recognized by the United Nations as one of the defining environmental crises of the 21st century. Rapid urbanization and unsustainable domestic consumption place extreme strain on municipal water reservoirs and depleting groundwater aquifers. Many households remain unaware of the specific fixtures contributing most heavily to their water waste due to lack of itemized consumption visibility.

The AquaSave Solution

AquaSave is an interactive, client-side web application designed to eliminate this awareness deficit. By decomposing household water usage into distinct activity modules, AquaSave calculates accurate consumption estimates, pinpoints the primary water-consuming fixture, and dynamically calculates customized conservation interventions with quantified volumetric savings.

Technical Implementation

Engineered using pure HTML5, Vanilla CSS3, and JavaScript (ES6+) with zero external third-party script dependencies. All calculations, dynamic SVG chart renderings, what-if simulations, and persistent progress logging (via browser localStorage) operate entirely client-side, ensuring complete offline availability and data privacy.

Environmental Studies Objectives

1. Quantify individual domestic water footprints against per-capita benchmarks.
2. Promote zero-cost habit changes and low-cost hardware retrofits.
3. Illustrate the water-energy nexus through interactive what-if simulation.
4. Deliver an engaging, presentation-ready working prototype for academic evaluation.
